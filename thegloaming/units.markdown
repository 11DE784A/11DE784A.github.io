# Units

## Edran's Slimy Followers

_Slime Regular, Light Infantry_

| Attack: | +3 | Defence:   | 12 |
| Power:  | +1 | Toughness: | 14 |
| Morale: | +3 | Size:      | d4 |

___Split.___ When this unit is diminished, place an identical unit with the
same current combat statistics and casualties in an empty adjacent space.

___Corrode.___ When this unit makes a successful Attack test against an opposed
unit, that unit takes -2 to Attack and Defense. Each opposed unit can be
affected by this trait only once per battle.

## Jake's Village Men

_Human Regular, Medium Infantry_

| Attack: | +3 | Defence:   | 13 |
| Power:  | +2 | Toughness: | 12 |
| Morale: | +2 | Size:      | d8 |

___Courageous.___ Once per battle, this unit can choose to succeed on a Morale
check it just failed.
