## Acolyte Jack

_Small Humanoid (Kobold, Retainer), Any Alignment_

- __AC__ 15 (medium armor and shield)
- __Initiative__ +0 (10)
- __HP__ Eight times their level (number of d10 Hit Dice equal to their level)
- __Speed__ 30 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|

- __Skills__ Athletics +3 plus PB, History +1 plus PB, Perception +0 plus PB
- __Saving Throws__ All
- __Senses__ Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10 plus PB
- __Languages__ Common, Draconic

___Signature Attack (Gladius).___ Melee Weapon Attack: +3 plus PB to hit, reach
5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d8 plus PB piercing damage. Beginning at 7th level,
Jack can make this attack twice, instead of once, when they take the
Attack action on their turn.

___3rd Level: Reinforce (3/Day).___ When Jack sees an ally within 30
feet of them being targeted by an attack, Jack can use a reaction to
move up to their speed toward the target. If Jack ends their movement
within 5 feet of the target, the target gains a +PB bonus to their AC until the
beginning of Jack's next turn, including against the triggering attack.

___5th Level: Shield Bash (3/Day).___ Jack attempts to topple a Medium
or smaller creature within 5 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 10
plus PB Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. Succeed or fail, Jack
then makes two signature attacks against any creature within reach.

___7th Level: One-Kobold Army (1/Day).___ Jack moves up to their speed,
then makes a number of signature attacks equal to the number of enemies within
5 feet of them (minimum of three attacks). Each enemy within range must be
attacked at least once. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC 10 plus PB
Charisma saving throw or be frightened of Jack for 1 minute (save ends
at end of turn).

## Green Knight Torosh

- __AC__ 18 (heavy armor and shield)
- __Initiative__ +0 (10)
- __HP__ Eight times their level (number of d10 Hit Dice equal to their level)
- __Speed__ 30 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|

- __Skills__ Athletics +3 plus PB, Nature +2 plus PB, Religion +2 plus PB
- __Saving Throws__ All
- __Senses__ Passive Perception 12
- __Languages__ Common, Sylvan

___Signature Attack.___ Melee Weapon Attack: +3 plus PB to hit, reach 5 ft., one
target. Hit: 1d8 plus PB slashing damage. Beginning at 7th level, the knight can
make this attack twice, instead of once, when they take the Attack action on
their turn.

___3rd Level: Twisting Vines (3/Day).___ The knight makes a signature attack.
On a hit, the target is restrained. It can make a DC 11 plus PB Strength
(Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check on its turn to escape. If it
escapes, the vines wither and die.

___5th Level: Entangle (3/Day).___ 

___7th Level: Moonbeam (1/Day).___
